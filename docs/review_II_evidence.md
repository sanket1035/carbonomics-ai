# Carbonomics-AI: implementation evidence

Numbers here are regenerated by `python scripts/run_pipeline.py`; see `outputs/` for the
current files. The previous version of this document quoted sample predictions that do
not exist in the repository and was replaced.

## Module 1: Carbon accounting
- `src/emission_factors.py`: every factor has source, version, unit and a `verified` flag
  (values from the KKWIEER Master Data FY2025-26). Factors flagged `verified=False` are not
  yet confirmed.
- `src/calculations.py`: emission = activity x factor.
- `src/process_dataset.py`: weekly Scope 1 (generator diesel) and Scope 2 (electricity).
  Scope 3 is not measured weekly and is excluded.

## Module 2: Data and PostgreSQL
- Real data: `data/real/real_monthly_2025.csv` (12 monthly totals, Energy team log).
- Weekly dataset: SYNTHETIC, 52 weeks, calibrated to the monthly totals (`data/synthetic/`).
- Validation: `src/clean_data.py` (columns, nulls, negatives, duplicates, 7-day steps).
- PostgreSQL: `database/schema.sql` (weekly_activity, forecast_results), upserts in
  `src/database/db_manager.py`, check with `scripts/verify_db.py`. Tested on a local
  PostgreSQL 16: 52 and 80 rows, unchanged after a second run. Needs DB_* variables
  (`.env.example`) and psycopg2.

## Module 3: Machine learning
- `src/ml/forecast_weekly.py`: weekly activity forecast, time-based split, naive and mean
  baselines, MAE/RMSE/R2. Details and results: `docs/ml_model_comparison.md`.

## QA
- `src/validation/qa_validator.py` computes every status; `outputs/qa_report.md`.
- `pytest tests`.
